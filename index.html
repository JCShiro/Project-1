Epic The Musical

What is Epic?

Epic the Musical is a concept musical album created by Jorge Rivera-Herrans. It is based on Homer's greek tale "The Odyssey", which follows Odysseus, the main character, on his journey home after "The Trojan war".
The musical is split up into different sections, called sagas, that help to depict the different stages on the story Jorge is trying to tell.
The musical picks up at the end of the trojan war, beginning in the trojan horse.
Jorge started writing Epic in June 2019 and released the final saga on 25th December 2024.

Jorge Rivera-Herrans

Jorge is the creator of Epic. He was raised in Puerto Rico and studied at the university of Notre Dame.
While studying at the university, he wrote the musical "My Heart Says Go" and began to work on epic for his senior thesis.

Troy saga

The Troy saga is the first installment of the musical, and picks up right towards the end of the trojan war, with Odysseus and his crew in the trojan horse on their way to the final fight in Troy.
The saga strongly sets up important characters such as Odysseus, Polities, Eurylochus and Athena quickly without detracting from the story.
The songs in this saga are "The Horse and the Infant", "Just a Man", "Full Speed Ahead", "Open Arms" and "Warrior of the Mind".

Cyclops saga

The Cyclops saga is the secon album to be released, and takes place in the cave of the cyclops Polyphemus. The album goes details how the characters deal with the conflict against the cyclops in a new and interesting way with engaging music and surprising twists.
This album includes the songs "Polyphemus", "Survive", "Remember Them" and "My Goodbye".

Ocean saga

The Ocean saga is the 3rd release for the musical, and follows the characters as they try to overcome a storm on their way home. The album introduces 2 gods to the story, Aeolus, the wind god, and Poseidon, who needs no introduction. Like the other albums, this one is filled with great tunes that are almost impossible to pick a favorite from.
The songs in this release are "Storm", "Luck Runs Out", "Keep Your Friends Close" and "Ruthlessness".

Circe saga

The fourth installment, the Circe saga, continues on the journey as the characters land on Circe's island. The saga introduces my personal favorite character - Hermes - as well as the minor godess, Circe.
The album is packed, like all the others, with amazing songs and the whole album consists of "Puppeteer", "Wouldn't You Like", "Done For" and "There Are Other Ways".

Underworld saga

Following their encounter with Circe, the Odysseus and the crew find their way into the underworld, which is where the 5th album picks up.
The Underworld saga follows the crew through the underworld in the shortest album of the musical. Through their journey in the underworld they make their way to Tiresias - the blind prophet, while also overcoming the obstacles of the past.
The album consists of only 3 songs, which are all masterpieces, "The Underworld", "No Longer You" and "monster".
