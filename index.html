Epic The Musical

What is Epic?

Epic the Musical is a concept musical album created by Jorge Rivera-Herrans. It is based on Homer's greek tale "The Odyssey", which follows Odysseus, the main character, on his journey home after "The Trojan war".
The musical is split up into different sections, called sagas, that help to depict the different stages on the story Jorge is trying to tell.
The musical picks up at the end of the trojan war, beginning in the trojan horse.
Jorge started writing Epic in June 2019 and released the final saga on 25th December 2024.

Jorge Rivera-Herrans

Jorge is the creator of Epic. He was raised in Puerto Rico and studied at the university of Notre Dame.
While studying at the university, he wrote the musical "My Heart Says Go" and began to work on epic for his senior thesis.

Troy saga

The Troy saga is the first installment of the musical, and picks up right towards the end of the trojan war, with Odysseus and his crew in the trojan horse on their way to the final fight in Troy.
The saga strongly sets up important characters such as Odysseus, Polities, Eurylochus and Athena quickly without detracting from the story.
The songs in this saga are "The Horse and the Infant", "Just a Man", "Full Speed Ahead", "Open Arms" and "Warrior of the Mind".

Cyclops saga

The Cyclops saga is the secon album to be released, and takes place in the cave of the cyclops Polyphemus. The album goes details how the characters deal with the conflict against the cyclops in a new and interesting way with engaging music and surprising twists.
This album includes the songs "Polyphemus", "Survive", "Remember Them" and "My Goodbye".

Ocean saga

The Ocean saga is the 3rd release for the musical, and follows the characters as they try to overcome a storm on their way home. The album introduces 2 gods to the story, Aeolus, the wind god, and Poseidon, who needs no introduction. Like the other albums, this one is filled with great tunes that are almost impossible to pick a favorite from.
The songs in this release are "Storm", "Luck Runs Out", "Keep Your Friends Close" and "Ruthlessness".

Circe saga

The fourth installment, the Circe saga, continues on the journey as the characters land on Circe's island. The saga introduces my personal favorite character - Hermes - as well as the minor godess, Circe.
The album is packed, like all the others, with amazing songs and the whole album consists of "Puppeteer", "Wouldn't You Like", "Done For" and "There Are Other Ways".

Underworld saga

Following their encounter with Circe, the Odysseus and the crew find their way into the underworld, which is where the 5th album picks up.
The Underworld saga follows the crew through the underworld in the shortest album of the musical. Through their journey in the underworld they make their way to Tiresias - the blind prophet, while also overcoming the obstacles of the past.
The album consists of only 3 songs, which are all masterpieces, "The Underworld", "No Longer You" and "monster".

Thunder saga

The 6th installment picks up not long after the crew have exited the underworld. When we rejoin them, they have aquired a new ship and set sail to find Poseidon. The crew encounter sirens and Odysseus tricks them into telling them a way home before killing them. From the sirens, the crew find out their only way home is through.. the lair of Scylla...
The album consists of "Suffering", "Different Beast", "Scylla", "Mutiny" and "Thunder Bringer".

Wisdom saga

The 7th saga, the Wisodm Saga carrys on 7 years after the events of "Thunder Bringer" in Ithica, where we are introduced to Telemachus, Odysseus' son (Voiced by one of my favorite artists, MICO). In the first song "Legendary he talks about growing up in Ithica without his father, and then ends the song running into the suitors. They then fight and Athena appears, helping Telemachus fight the suitors. Athena then, by herself, looks back to find out where Odysseus is, soon finding out he is trapped on an island.
This album consists of "Legendary", "Little Wolf", "We'll Be Fine", "Love in Paradise" and lastly "God Games".

Vengence saga

The Vengence Saga carrys on with Odysseus' journey as he departs from the island he was trapped on, meeting Hermes who guides him home. The 8th release of the musical then goes on to detail Charybdis and how Odysseus overcomes that monster. The saga then explores Poseidon's return at the Ithican coast.
The albus contains the songs "Not Sorry for Loving You", "Dangerous", "Charybdis", "Get In the Water" and lastly "Six Hundred Strike".

Ithica saga

The Ithica Saga is the final installment of the musical. At the very opening we are finally introduced to Penelope, Odysseus' wife, and how she has kept the suitors at bay for 20 years, but they are now getting restless and begin to plot to take the throne in the darkest song of the album "Hold Them Down". Odysseus then returns to Ithica in a bloody reveal and is soon reunited with his family.
The final release consists of "The Challenge", "Hold Them Down", "Odysseus", "I Can't Help But Wonder" and "Would You Fall In Love With Me Again".

The winions!!

The Winions are a unique touch for the Epic community and are Jorge's own design. The winions appear in "Open arms" as the Lotus eater winions. They next appear in "Keep Your Friends Close" as wind sprite winios (aka minios of Aeolus) and then have a final brief cameo in "Dangerous" for a few lines of song.
The fan base took an immediate love to winios and now they are the official logo for the Epic the Musical Merch.

Favourite song and character

As guessed by the image above, my favorite character is Hermes, the messenger god, as well as the god of Theives, Trade, Roads, Travelers and many more. Another fact, not mentioned in Epic, is that he is Odysseus' great grandfather. I personally think Epic gives Hermes such a cool and chilled out vibe that even when everything seems as though it's falling appart he'll still continue to party out all the problems.
Subsequently, my favorite songs are the messenger gods 2 songs, "Would'nt You Like" and "Dangerous". For me it was almost impossible to pick between these 2 songs as they both have such a unique vibe that I absoloutley just love.
